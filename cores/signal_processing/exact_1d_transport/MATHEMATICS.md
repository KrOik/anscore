# Exact integer contract and proof

For ordered signed integer coordinates x_0<...<x_(B-1), unsigned masses a_i,b_i and equal totals T, choose nonnegative integer pi with row sums a and column sums b to minimize sum pi_ij*abs(x_i-x_j). Ascending residual matching skips zero bins, chooses q=min(residual_a,residual_b)>0, emits q, and advances every exhausted pointer after retirement. Equal residuals advance both. This fixes a canonical plan; uniqueness of every L1 optimum is not claimed.

For each adjacent-coordinate cut, D_k=sum_(i<=k)(a_i-b_i). Any feasible plan must carry at least abs(D_k) units across that cut, so every cost is at least sum_(k<B-1) abs(D_k)*(x_(k+1)-x_k). Ascending matching never has opposing crossings on the same cut, attains this bound and is optimal. If p and q bins have positive source/target mass, every emitted flow exhausts one residual and the last exhausts both, hence flows<=p+q-1<=2B-1. There are no zero-quantity tokens; positive diagonal flows are retained.

Let L=ceil(log2 B), TOTAL_W=C+L and COST_W=C+L+X. T<=B*(2^C-1)<2^TOTAL_W. Coordinate difference is formed after sign extension to the shared COST_W+1-bit path, which is at least X+1; ordered larger minus smaller is nonnegative and at most 2^X-1. Its unsigned magnitude fits X bits. q fits C bits; the product fits C+X bits. Final cost<=T*(2^X-1)<2^COST_W. Running costs and every partial serial product are nonnegative and no larger than this bound. Therefore explicit low-width writeback loses no significant bit. No saturation, rounding, normalization or modulo semantics is used.

The shared adder/subtractor is COST_W+1 bits. Totals, residuals, distance, partial product and accumulated cost are extended BEFORE arithmetic. Serial multiplication takes exactly C shifts/additions including zero multiplier bits; it does not use a parallel `*` operator. Indices have max(1,clog2 B) bits, pointers/capture counts have max(1,clog2(B+1)) bits so B is representable, and flow count has max(1,clog2(2B)) bits so 2B-1 is representable.

Future preparation independently computes (1) the cut sum, and (2) all B^2 cumulative interval overlaps max(0,min(A_i,B_j)-max(A_(i-1),B_(j-1))) using arbitrary-precision integer Python. The latter gives canonical cells without reproducing the RTL pointer FSM. It checks marginals, positive quantities, lexicographic order, total quantity, cost, hand targets and running cost/count. Invalid frames have diagnostic-only summaries. This model is unexecuted at source authoring. Later HDL and raw parser compare supplied stored tuples; they do not recompute cuts, interval overlaps or another optimizer.
