# backemf_obs_s12

One step of a back-EMF sliding observer for sensorless speed, one beat, stateful. Measured voltage v=a[11:0] and current i=a[23:12] are signed 12-bit counts, the winding-resistance gain R=b[11:0] is signed 12-bit; a[31:24] and b[31:12] must be zero. c[3:0]=sR shifts the IR product, c[7:4]=sF shifts the speed word, c[11:8]=lambda is the leakage exponent, c[30]=1 freezes the integrator, c[31]=1 restarts it to zero and seeds the beat counter; c[29:12] must be zero (malformed beat: result=aux=0, fault=1, state kept). drop=round_up(i*R>>>sR), emf=clamp12(v-drop) clipped to the signed 12-bit domain [-2048,2047], then acc_next=clamp12(acc+emf-(acc>>>lambda)) with acc=state[11:0] (the integral of EMF, the flux-linkage estimate) and speed=round_up(acc_next>>>sF). A constant emf E converges exactly to the first acc with floor(acc/2^lambda)=E, so the speed word is that estimate scaled by 2^lambda. state[11:0]=acc, state[19:12]=beats since restart (saturates at 255). result[11:0]=speed, result[12]=integrator rail, result[13]=emf clipped, result[14]=freeze echo, result[15]=restart echo, result[19:16]=lambda, result[23:20]=sR, result[31:24]=0. aux[11:0]=emf, aux[23:12]=acc, aux[31:24]=beat count. fault=1 on a rail or clip it had to apply, or on a malformed beat.

See [common interface](../../doc/INTERFACE.md). Latency: 1 enabled edge(s); II=1 when output ready. Independent lanes per accepted beat: 1. Only accepted transactions update state. Reset clears pending output and state, even while CE is zero.

Hardware advantage: Speed from EMF is a subtract, a gain product and a leaky accumulate: a CPU ISR samples it after the PWM edge with a control-loop delay of one whole period, while this advances the estimate on the same enabled beat the converter is commutated.

Independent integer model: `step("backemf_obs_s12", a, b, c, state)` in tools/fam_motor.py. Vector rows: 320, of which 128 run on the board. Board-set coverage: 126 distinct responses, 121 fault rows.
