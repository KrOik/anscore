# ctl_pid_form_s16

Two fixed-structure PID controllers, no multipliers. state[15:0]=previous error lane0, state[31:16]=previous error lane1 (signed 16), state[63:32]=integral accumulator lane0 and state[95:64]=integral accumulator lane1 (signed 32, clamped to plus or minus (2^23-1)). a[15:0]=error lane0, a[31:16]=error lane1. Shift gains, all arithmetic right shifts of two-complement values: b[3:0]=sP gives proportional term e>>>sP, b[7:4]=sI gives integral increment e>>>sI, b[11:8]=sD gives derivative term (e-e_prev)>>>sD, b[15:12]=sO is the output shift, b[18:16]=sK sets the derivative-kick threshold 1<<(15-sK). b[31:19] ignored. c[31]=1 restarts: the integral begins at zero before this beat increment, the derivative term is forced to zero and the stored error is re-baselined. c[30]=1 freezes the integral accumulator. Other c bits ignored. Per lane acc := clamp(acc + (e>>>sI), -(2^23-1), 2^23-1) unless frozen or restarted, then u := (e>>>sP) + acc + ((e-e_prev)>>>sD), u := u>>>sO and finally u clamped to signed 16. result=u with lane0 in [15:0] and lane1 in [31:16]. aux[1:0]=lanes whose integral clamped, aux[3:2]=lanes whose output clamped, aux[5:4]=lanes with a derivative kick, aux[7:6]=lanes with a non-zero derivative term, aux[9:8]=lanes whose integral accumulator is negative, aux[10]=restart applied, aux[11]=integral frozen, aux[12]=both accumulators zero, aux[27:16]=b[15:4] gain config echo, other bits 0. fault=derivative kick on a restart beat, that is a lane whose absolute error change reached the threshold at the moment its derivative history was thrown away; saturation only sets aux flags. hysteresis32 and stream_credit32 in the sibling library are single-window compare or ledger cores; this is a two-lane controller form with windup accounting.

See [common interface](../../doc/INTERFACE.md). Latency: 1 enabled edge(s); II=1 when output ready. Independent lanes per accepted beat: 2. Only accepted transactions update state. Reset clears pending output and state, even while CE is zero.

Hardware advantage: A dual-axis loop needs three shift-scaled terms, an anti-windup accumulator and a kick detector every cycle; the fabric adds them in one beat with no multiply and no sampling jitter.

Independent integer model: `step("ctl_pid_form_s16", a, b, c, state)` in tools/fam_control.py. Vector rows: 320, of which 128 run on the board. Board-set coverage: 122 distinct responses, 19 fault rows.
