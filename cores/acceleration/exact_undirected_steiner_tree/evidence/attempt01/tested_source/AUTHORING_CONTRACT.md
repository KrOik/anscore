## Mathematical state and safe arithmetic

D[S,v] is the minimum distinct-edge cost of a connected undirected subgraph containing vertex v and all runtime terminals in nonempty subset S; otherwise invalid. Positive original edge weights only. Singletons seed0 at their terminal. For every numeric mask in increasing order, initialize every current-mask row, merge all proper nonempty submasks A in ascending numeric order with A < (S xor A) to omit duplicate unordered partitions, then perform complete dense multi-source Dijkstra closure. Child masks are proper and immutable. DP valid bit is separate from numeric score.

Dijkstra selects minimum valid unsettled distance, lowest vertex ID on equal distance. Mark selected vertex settled before relaxing ascending target IDs. Relax only unsettled targets, only present edges, and only strict improvements; parent is therefore settled/immutable and MOVE cost strictly decreases toward parent. MERGE stores the first retained split; no equal-cost replacement. Final root is lowest vertex ID attaining the minimum valid full-mask cost. No global lexicographic witness claim.

Let SW=W+ceil(log2 N). Every optimal positive tree is simple with <=N-1 edges and fits SW. Both merge and move candidates use zero-extended SW+1 addition/comparison; if high carry is1, discard the candidate before any SW narrowing. A feasible optimum always fits, so discarding cannot remove an optimum. A high transient candidate must never wrap into a low score. The overflow fixture includes terminals0,6,7: at root0 the {6}/{7} merge trial is12M, but rooted/full optimum7M via hub5 fits. The B review's terminals6,7 alone have unrooted optimum2M; adding terminal0 makes7M the full-case target.

Every descriptor candidate expands into a connected feasible edge union with distinct cost<=descriptor sum. Conversely optimal rooted positive tree decomposes into a proper terminal partition at a branch plus a path, or a singleton path, proving recurrence exact. At final optimum union cost<=DP optimum and feasibility implies>=DP optimum, hence equality. Duplicated positive edge occurrences or a positive cycle would give a strictly cheaper feasible union, contradiction. Thus final union is a tree, its distinct edge sum equals exact optimum, and selected edges<=N-1. Positive-weight restriction is essential to this simple proof.
